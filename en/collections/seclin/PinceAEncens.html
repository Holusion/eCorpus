Description

These copper alloy tongs were used to handle the burning charcoal needed to burn incense. Their rectangular arms end in chamfered tips and their spring forms an omega shape.

They were found near a hexagonal monumental fountain built in the 16th century, probably during the provostship of Jean II Carondelet, between 1520 and 1545. The object was used for private prayer: it allowed the user to handle the embers required to burn incense.

Personalised by its owner, a Gothic Latin inscription runs along both arms of the tongs: DONA NOBIS PACEM (“Grant us peace”), a formula introduced into the Catholic liturgy by Pope Sergius I in 687.
