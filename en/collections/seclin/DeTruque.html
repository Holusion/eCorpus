Description

This die, made of hard animal material, has edges smoothed with a file and numbers incised as concentric circles. It was found in the fill of the latrines of the Cheval Blanc tavern, an establishment open between 1536 and 1846 in the heart of the canonical quarter of Seclin.

Made during the 19th century, it was tampered with: the “2” is missing, disguised as a “3” by adding a third mark between the first two. While 4-2-1 gave pride of place to a three-of-a-kind on a loaded die used by a cheat, that combination meant a loss in the game of Passe-Dix on a loaded die passed on to an opponent. Both dice games were very popular in 19th-century French taverns.
