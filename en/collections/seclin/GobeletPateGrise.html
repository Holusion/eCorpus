Description

This grey ware ceramic goblet with a thin wall was found in the fill of a latrine attached to the house of a canon of the Saint-Piat chapter.

Produced and used during the 14th and 15th centuries, it documents the daily life of the canonical quarter at the end of the Middle Ages.
