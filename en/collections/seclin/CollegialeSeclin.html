Global Characteristics

Title:

Saint-Piat Collegiate Church

Location:

Seclin (Nord), France

Date of construction:

12th century (current building), on a sanctuary founded in the 7th century

Collection:

Archaeological collection of the city of Seclin

Description

The Saint-Piat collegiate church towers over the town of Seclin; it is one of the oldest religious buildings in northern France.

Saint Eligius, bishop of Noyon and Tournai, had the collegiate church built in the 7th century over the tomb of Saint Piat, a martyr beheaded in Seclin in 287.
