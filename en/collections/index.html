Digitalized elements from the Cloister Museum of Notre-Dame-en-Vaux

Collection assembled for the Cloister Museum of Notre-Dame-en-Vaux in Châlons-en-Champagne for its 2025 reopening.

Scans were produced by Digitage while reconstructions of missing parts were done by Holusion.

Masterpieces from Hauts-de-France high schools — Résidence Pro 2026 at the Villa Medici

Collection of works from 9 participating schools and the Compagnons du Devoir, part of an Erasmus+ project involving 250 students and 50 supervisors.

Most scans were done by students using Scaniverse. Art metalwork, too complex for students, was scanned by Holusion using photogrammetry.

INRAP, Hauts-de-France

Collection of archaeological objects from the Medieval era to the Modern era, discovered by INRAP Hauts-de-France in Dunkirk, France.

The objects were selected for an exhibition at an event in Dunkirk in 2026. They were scanned the same year by the Holusion team.
