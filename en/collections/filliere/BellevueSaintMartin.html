Name

Statue portraying Saint Martin

Origins

Unknown

Date

16th century.

Place of conservation

Church of Saint-Martin-Bellevue, Haute-Savoie, France.

Dimensions

height = 89 cm.

Materials

Polychromed wood.

Palissy File

https://pop.culture.gouv.fr/notice/palissy/PM74000355

Description

The figure stands upright as a bishop: mitre on his head, red cope lined with blue over a white alb, stole crossed on the chest. The left hand is drawn against the body and the right arm is missing at the wrist. He stands on a moulded hexagonal base highlighted with gold. The red, blue and off-white polychromy is well preserved.
